**What I want to build with AI:**
AI Personal Day and Meal Planner: An intelligent assistant that creates personalised daily routines and meal plans based on an individual's lifestyle, age, location, dietary preferences, health goals, budget, local food availability, and even weather conditions. It could also manage shopping lists, estimate quantities based on household size, suggest new recipes, and plan restocking.

AI-Powered Product Distribution and Warehouse Management: A system that helps businesses manage products across multiple warehouses, distributors, and locations. It would track inventory, monitor stock levels, process incoming orders, and automatically notify the nearest warehouse or distributor to fulfil orders based on customer location.
